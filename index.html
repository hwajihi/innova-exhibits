Innova Exhibits is an Exhibition & Brand Activation Studio based in Nairobi. We design stands, produce collateral, and supply merchandise — for international companies exhibiting in Kenya and local brands looking to make their mark. One team. One brief. One invoice.

We're not a print shop. Not a builder. Not an event company. We're the single partner that connects all three — so nothing falls out of tune across your exhibition presence.

03 — Services

Everything your brand
needs to show up.

From the floor plan to the finished merchandise — whether you're flying in from abroad or exhibiting locally, one relationship handles everything.

🎨

Design

Brand identity, stand layout concepts, artwork production, and print-ready files. Considered design that translates your brief into a coherent visual presence.

🏗️

Booth Panel Design

Shell schemes transformed into branded spaces. Half panels, full panels, and seamless walls — sticker, matte banner, forex, fabric, or lightbox. We also supply carpet and vinyl flooring to complete the stand.

🎪

Advertising Displays

Roll-up banners, teardrop flags, and custom branded gazebo tents. High-visibility formats for expos, activations, and outdoor events.

🖨️

Print Media

Brochures, flyers, and business cards — produced to spec, on brand, ready for the floor. Print that earns its keep in the room.

🎁

Branded Merchandise

Pens, notebooks, mugs, T-shirts, hats, keychains, and more. Giveaways that carry your brand beyond the event day.

🪑

Rentals

Shelves, TVs, tables, chairs, stools, sofas, pedestals, fake plants, and magazine racks. Everything the stand needs, furnished.

📷

Photography & Videography

Professional documentation of your stand and activation — wide architectural shots, craft detail photography, and event video. Content that works long after the expo closes.
